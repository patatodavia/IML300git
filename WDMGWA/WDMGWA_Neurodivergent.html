NEURODIVERGENCY


fluffballkitten : About the movie Rain Man -> "When this came out it became the standard that most nonexpert people thought of for autism. So for a long time nonexpert people thought if you didn't act like this, you couldn't possibly be autistic. I think this contributed to me not getting diagnosed as a kid. So not a fan"

Kitty-Moo : "Same, for a lot of people this was their only example of what autism looked like for the longest time. So when I was struggling with pretty much every aspect of school, no one thought about autism. It just wasn't part of the discussion. I didn't fit our only known example."

Geminii27 : "The character wasn't even based off an autistic person. But for years it was the only example in media that most people had of someone neurodivergent, so it was the singular go-to reference for everything."

Elalamyn : "I just want someone (either someone that does or doesn't have autism) represent the community in a well written way. I don't want some rain man movie where it seems like everyone with autism is someone completely closed off from society."

IvanEd747 : "Either represent it accurately or don’t say it’s ASD. I don’t mind who the actor is as long as the portrayal is accurate."
