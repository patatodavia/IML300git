This webiste was created by myself, Davia as a way to higlight the misrepresentation of diverse communities in media based on Reddit Comments.

This project was made to reflect the way in which media often misrepresents diverse communities, whether that be through inaccurate locations, poor casting choices, or lack of respect for the culture being portrayed. The sticky words above represent some of the key aspects that are often overlooked in media portrayals. By highlighting these issues, I hope to encourage more authentic and respectful representations in the future. All of the examples used in these posts are from reddit channels that talk about the way that movies and other media can misrepresent certain groups of people and how that can leave lasting affects on the way that these communities are thought of. I hope that media makers will use this page to better understand what they can do to help these communities be more understood.
